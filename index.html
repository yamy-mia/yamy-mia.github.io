Writing

Notes from building and learning.

AI · Sales · E-commerce

I stopped trying to automate everything.

When I moved from e-commerce operations into sales, the problems changed — from content workflows to prospecting, lead organization, outreach and follow-ups. I built CNN Sales around that process, and initially assumed the best version would automate as much as possible.

In practice, research-heavy automation can mean more API calls, more cost and less visibility into what is happening. I found a better balance: use the AI tools I already subscribe to for research and analysis, review the output myself, then move the useful information into my workflow for execution and tracking.

The goal isn't to automate everything. It's to automate the right things.
